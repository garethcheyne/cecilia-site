# The Cecilia guide

Cecilia records what your organist plays, keeps it in a library, and plays it back through the organ, stops and all. This guide is written for organists and the people who help them, not for computer experts.

## Where to start

- **Trying it out?** Read [Getting started](/docs/getting-started/). The built-in simulator means you don't need an organ.
- **Setting it up at church?** Read [Connecting your organ](/docs/connecting-your-organ/), then [Setting up your organ](/docs/your-organ/), then [Raspberry Pi](/docs/raspberry-pi/).
- **Running the music on Sunday?** Read [Playlists and services](/docs/services/).

## What Cecilia can do

- Record a whole service with one press, saving each piece on its own.
- Play any recording back on your organ, or on a different one.
- Plan services as playlists, with verse control, and run them from a large-text screen, across two screens if you have them.
- Find your organ by maker and model from about 200 ready-made setups.
- Show music as notation, and edit it.
- Import MIDI, MusicXML and scanned printed music.
- Share hymns and organ setups with other churches.
