# Getting started

You can try everything in Cecilia on a laptop, with no organ, using the **simulator**: a virtual organ with sound, built in.

## 1. Install and open it

- **Windows:** download the latest release, unzip it, and run `cecilia.exe`. A window shows Cecilia's messages; leave it open.
- **Raspberry Pi:** see [Raspberry Pi](/docs/raspberry-pi/).

Then open **http://127.0.0.1:7070** in your web browser. That's Cecilia: everything happens in the browser, on the same computer or on a phone or tablet on the same network.

## 2. Choose the simulator

Open **MIDI Setup** and choose **Cecilia Simulator** as both **Organ in** and **Organ out**, then **Save & connect**. Cecilia now records from, and plays into, the virtual organ.

## 3. Play something

Open **Simulator**. Touch some stops to draw them (they light up) and play the on-screen keys, or use your computer keyboard: the row from **A** to **;** plays the notes, and **Z** and **X** change octave.

## 4. Record and play back

1. Press **Record** in the bar at the bottom. It says *Waiting for the first note*.
2. Play a short tune. When you stop, Cecilia waits for a few seconds of silence, then saves it.
3. Open **Library**: your recording is at the top. Press **Play** and watch the stops and keys move by themselves.

## 5. Try a hymn

The library comes with hymns such as *All People That on Earth Do Dwell* and *Abide with Me*. Play one, and try the controls in the bottom bar: pause, tempo, **End of verse**, **Last verse** and **Fade**.
