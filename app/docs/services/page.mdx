# Playlists and services

A **playlist** is the order of service: the music, in order, with text lines such as *Readings* or *Sermon* in between.

## Making one

1. **Playlists**, then **New playlist**. It suggests next Sunday's date.
2. **Add music** and tick pieces in the order you want them.
3. For each piece, choose how it **Starts**:
   - **When you press Play**: it's ready, and starts when someone presses Play.
   - **By itself**: it starts after the pause you set (useful for a run of voluntaries).
   - **Ready at the first note**: the stops are set and it waits at the first note, so Play starts the music instantly.
4. **Verses**: type `4` to play four verses, or a list like `1, 2, 5` to play exactly those. Leave it empty for all of them.
5. A **Note** shows beside the piece, for example *Hymn 477, page 312*.

Changes save themselves. **Repeat**, **Shuffle** and **Silent mode** (leave the stops drawn when a piece ends) are at the top.

## Running the service

Press **Run service** (in the playlist, or on the **Overview** under *Next service*) to open the **Service** screen: large text, the current piece highlighted, the next one marked, and big buttons. **Space** plays and pauses; the **right arrow** goes to the next piece.

- **Tap** a piece to make it the next one.
- **Double-click** it (on a phone or tablet, **double-tap**) to play it straight away.

If no service is running, the **Service** screen lists your playlists, each with its own **Run service** button.

If a piece was edited after it was added to a playlist, it shows **Edited**, so nobody is surprised on Sunday. Saving the playlist clears the warning.

## Using more than one screen

Many consoles have two screens, or one wide one. Press **Displays** on the **Service** screen to choose what each shows:

- **Order of service:** the pieces in order, with Play, Next and Stop.
- **Music:** the score, following along as it plays.
- **Now playing:** a large title, the verse, the time left and what's next, readable from a distance.
- **Organ console:** the stops and keys, live.

For **this screen** and a **second screen**, choose **One panel** or **Two side by side**, and which panels go where. Your choice is remembered on that device.

To use a second screen, press **Open on second screen**. Edge and Chrome can tell when a second screen is plugged in and put the window there for you (they ask permission the first time). Otherwise press **Open in a new window**, drag the window onto the other screen, and press its **Full screen** button. Every screen follows the same service, so pressing Play on one is seen on all.

The **Full screen** button on the **Service** screen hides the menus on the screen you're using.
