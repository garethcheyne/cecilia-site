# Questions and answers

**Does Cecilia make sound itself?**
The organ makes the sound: Cecilia plays it the way an organist would. The built-in simulator has its own sound for practising and trying things out.

**Will it work with our organ?**
If your organ has MIDI sockets, almost certainly. **Organs → Find my organ** has setups for about 200 models from Johannus, Makin, Allen, Viscount, Rodgers and others, and you can teach it any organ with *Learn every stop*.

**Why does a template still need learning?**
Makers publish their stop lists, but not the MIDI message each stop sends. A template has the right stops, keyboards and pitches; *Learn every stop* teaches Cecilia which message is which stop, in about ten minutes. Share it afterwards and the next church with that model won't need to.

**Can it play music we didn't record?**
Yes. The library includes hymns to start with, you can import MIDI and MusicXML, and the shared library has more.

**What about CCLI and copyright?**
Your church's CCLI licence covers your own worship. Recording your organist for your own services is your church's business; check what your licence includes. Sharing copyrighted music publicly is not allowed, which is why the shared library only takes public-domain or openly licensed music.

**Can we share modern worship songs?**
No. Songs still in copyright can't be shared publicly, even if your church has a CCLI licence: it covers your own worship, not publishing. Keep them in your own library, under your licence.

**Who sees our organists' names?**
Nobody outside your church, unless a recording is shared, and then only for organists who've agreed in **Settings → Organists**. Otherwise a shared recording says *A Cecilia organist*.

**Is it really free?**
Yes, and open source under the MIT licence. There's no subscription or advertising, and no account is needed, except to share with other churches.

**Is Cecilia OrganAssist?**
No. Cecilia is a new, independent program inspired by David Glover's OrganAssist, written without its code. Recordings come across through OrganAssist's MIDI backup. See [About](/about/).

**What happens if the Wi-Fi goes down mid-service?**
Playback runs on the Cecilia box itself, so the music carries on; your phone just stops showing the controls until it reconnects.

**Can I say "Cecilia, play Abide with Me"?**
Not yet: voice control is planned. Cecilia can already be controlled by AI assistants that support MCP (see *Assistant* in the app).
