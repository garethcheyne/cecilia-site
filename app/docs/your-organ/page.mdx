# Setting up your organ

Cecilia needs to know your organ: its keyboards, its stops, and the MIDI message each one sends. That's an **organ setup**.

## Start from the closest one

Open **Organs**. Find your organ (or the closest template, such as **Makin 2-manual**) and press **Copy to set up**. Give it a name, and Cecilia opens it for editing. Press **Use this organ** to make it the one Cecilia talks to.

## Learn every stop

This is the important part, and it takes about ten minutes at the console:

1. Choose your MIDI interface as **Organ in** on **MIDI Setup**.
2. On your organ's page, press **Learn every stop**.
3. For each stop Cecilia names, **pull it out**, then **push it in**. Cecilia moves to the next by itself.
4. Press **Skip** for anything it doesn't need, and **Save** at the end.

A tablet or phone at the console makes this easy.

## Keyboards and pedals

On **Keyboards and pedals**:

- **Learn the keys:** play the lowest and highest note of each keyboard.
- **Learn a swell pedal:** move the pedal fully shut and fully open.
- **Learn the general cancel:** press the General Cancel piston. Organs whose stops toggle (Makin, Johannus) need this.

## Changing a stop by hand

The pencil beside a stop changes its name lines, what kind of stop it is (family and pitch, or which keyboard a coupler brings in), its shape, and its MIDI in hex, for anyone who knows their organ's MIDI chart.

Cecilia checks every setup when you save, and tells you plainly if two controls would answer the same message.
