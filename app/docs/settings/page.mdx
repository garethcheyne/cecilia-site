# Settings, password and backup

Everything is under **Settings**.

- **Recording:** how long a silence ends a piece, and how much silence to keep before and after.
- **Playback:** how long a fade takes, and the pause after a general cancel on organs whose stops toggle.
- **Text size:** Normal, Large or Larger, remembered for each device. A tablet at the console can use big text while a laptop doesn't.

## Organists

List the people who play at your church. For each one:

- **Name**, as it appears on their recordings.
- **Credit as:** how they'd like to be named publicly, for example *John Brown, St Mary's*.
- **OK to name them when a recording is shared:** off unless they've agreed. Without it, a shared recording says *A Cecilia organist*. Ask each organist before turning it on.

**Who's playing now** is the organist new recordings are credited to. You can also change it beside the **Record** button (on a phone, in the playback drawer). Names stay on this computer unless a recording is shared.

## Cecilia Community

Sign in here to share organ setups and music with other churches:

1. Type your email and press **Send code**.
2. Type the six-digit code from the email and press **Sign in**.

Add your **name** and **church**, which is how other churches see you. **What you've shared** lists everything you've sent and where it's got to: waiting for review, published, or not accepted (with the reason). See [Sharing music and organs](/docs/sharing/).

## Password

Until a password is set, anyone on the same network can use Cecilia. **Set one before connecting Cecilia to the church Wi-Fi.** Other devices then need it once; the device you set it from stays signed in.

## Backup and restore

**Download a backup** saves everything (music, playlists, organ setups, settings) in one file. **Restore from a backup** brings it back on the same or another Cecilia. Items with the same name are replaced; everything else stays. The device's MIDI ports and password aren't changed.

Cecilia also keeps the last 20 versions of every piece in its history folder, in case an edit goes wrong.
