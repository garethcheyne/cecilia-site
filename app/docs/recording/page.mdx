# Recording

Press **Record** in the bar at the bottom of any screen. Cecilia then:

1. **waits for the first note**, so you can press Record and walk to the console;
2. records the piece, starting with the **stops that were drawn** at that moment;
3. when the organ has been **silent for a while** (8 seconds unless you change it), saves the piece to the library and waits for the next.

So one press records a whole service, and each voluntary and hymn becomes its own piece, named with the date and time. Rename them in the library afterwards.

Stops changed *between* pieces become the starting registration of the next piece, so nothing is lost.

Press the red button again to stop. A piece being played at that moment is saved.

## Who's playing

Choose who's at the console beside the **Record** button (on a phone, in the playback drawer). Each new recording is then credited to them in **Played by**. Add organists in **Settings → Organists**; see [Settings](/docs/settings/).

## Good habits

- Press **General Cancel** on the organ before you start, especially on a Makin.
- If a long pause in the middle of a piece splits it in two, make the silence longer in **Settings**.
- The silence kept before and after each piece can be set in **Settings** too (handy when recording sound at the same time).
