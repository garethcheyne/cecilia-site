# Importing music

In the **Library**, press **Import** and choose a file. Cecilia shows what's inside before anything is added.

## MIDI files

- **Recorded on this organ, or an OrganAssist backup:** Cecilia reads the stops and pedals as well as the notes. It notices when a file matches your organ and suggests this for you.
- **From other software:** choose which keyboard each MIDI channel plays. If a file came from MuseScore, tick *Pedal notes an octave up*.
- **Long files:** tick *Split into separate pieces* to cut a whole service into pieces at the silences.

### Coming from OrganAssist

In OrganAssist, use **Backup Entire Library as MIDI**, then import those files into Cecilia with your organ selected. Your existing recordings come across, stops and all.

## MusicXML

From MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale or Dorico, export **MusicXML** (`.musicxml` or `.mxl`). Cecilia lists the parts (it sends the lowest voice to the pedal), and you can set the speed and how many verses to play.

## Scans and photos of printed music

Choose a PNG, JPEG or PDF of printed music, and Cecilia reads it with **Audiveris**, a free music recognition program installed alongside Cecilia. It works well on clean hymn books; check the result in the **Score** view before Sunday.
