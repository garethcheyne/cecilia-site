# Editing and notation

Open a piece from the **Library**: its menu has **Open in editor**.

## The piano roll

- **Notes** are bars coloured by keyboard (Great blue, Swell green, Pedal purple). Drag to move them, drag the right edge to lengthen them, and use the **up and down arrows** to transpose by a semitone (with Shift, an octave).
- **Draw** mode adds notes on the keyboard you choose.
- **Stop changes** are the dots (drawn) and crosses (pushed in) in the top lane; drag them to move them. Add one at the cursor from the panel.
- The **swell pedal** is the line beneath.
- **Verse markers** are the yellow flags at the bottom: double-click to add one in the gap between verses, drag to move.
- **Sections:** drag along the time ruler to select a passage, then **Copy**, **Cut out**, or clear just its notes or just its stops. **Paste section** and **Insert silence** work at the cursor. The clipboard is kept when you open another piece, so you can splice recordings together.

**Undo** (Ctrl+Z) and **Redo** (Ctrl+Y) cover everything. **Save** (Ctrl+S) keeps the previous version in the history folder. **Space** plays from the cursor.

## Notation

The **Score** tab shows the piece on staves (right hand, left hand and pedal), with one tab per verse, following along as it plays. Hymns and imported scores are exact. Live recordings are shown as closely as Cecilia can manage; set the speed and time signature until the bars line up.
