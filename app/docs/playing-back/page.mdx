# Playing back

Press **Play** next to any piece in the **Library**. Before the music starts, Cecilia clears the organ (on a Makin, with its general cancel, then a short pause for the stops to settle), then draws the opening registration.

## The controls

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Play / Pause** | Pause lifts the keys and holds your place, like a tape recorder; press again to carry on. |
| **Stop** | Stops and goes back to the start. |
| Position slider | Jump anywhere; the stops are set to how they were at that point. |
| **Tempo** | Slower or faster, 50 to 150%. **Reset to 100%** puts it back. |
| **End of verse** | Stops when the verse being played ends. |
| **Last verse** | When this verse ends, goes straight to the last one. |
| **Fade** | Brings the music down to silence and stops, then restores the volume. |

On a phone, tap the bar at the bottom to open all the controls.

## Playing on a different organ

Recordings remember what each stop *is* (Great Principal 8, Swell Oboe 8), not its MIDI. On another organ, Cecilia's **virtual organist** chooses the closest stops that organ has, and **never anything louder**: no Tuba for a missing Salicional. A part for a keyboard the organ doesn't have moves to another (in the end, the Great).
